You check one assistant reply before it is sent to the owner. Decide whether the reply claims that the assistant changed stored state when no listed effect backs that claim.

Stored state changes: adding, editing or removing a ledger row; saving, writing, editing or deleting a file or note; storing or updating a memory; creating or cancelling a routine or reminder; changing a setting; sending a message to someone else. A promise to keep information for later ("I'll remember that", "noted for next time", "set as your target") claims that it was stored. So does a bare acknowledgement such as "Noted." when the owner asked for something to be kept.

A claim is backed when an effect of the same kind on the same item is listed under this turn's effects or under the recent effects, which are what tools did earlier in this chat. Times and wording need not match exactly. A failed effect does not back a claim that it succeeded.

Not claims: repeating what the owner said; answering from the conversation; reading, searching or looking something up; quoting or summarizing stored data; reporting a failure or that nothing was saved; offers, questions and plans the owner has not asked to have kept.
