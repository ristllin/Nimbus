Do not edit by hand: re-run `GOLDEN_UPDATE=1 pio test -e native -f test_tx_budget` and read the diff.

Tokens are the unit, as the provider counts them (owner ruling 82). A head's request aims at about 100,000 tokens once the conversation can fill it, or at its own lower limit read from its released v5.1.2 row: the price tier (0.9 of it), the window (0.75 of it), v5.1.2's read-window fit, the routed floor while the lift is off. It never aims lower than v5.1.2 did. Compaction is due once a request reached the aim less the next request's growth (4,000 tokens: the largest growth in RB-M0b's aged replays was 3,774 inside a turn and 3,414 from turn to turn, 244 turns), and keeps 70% of the aim. Bytes stand beside the tokens only where a device limit is in bytes: the body cap and the stored marks H and L. They are the token marks on the head's wire: on the Anthropic Messages wire MEASURED (396 photo-free claude-sonnet-5-5 requests: body = 2.993 x tokens - 7,358 B), on every other wire v5.1.2's released nominal ratio; less the fixed part (18,432 B) and the digest block with the recent-files list (6,744 B). No default carries less than the head's v5.1.2 row (owner ruling 81): at every compaction the owner and assistant text kept is at least v5.1.2's keep.
