## Electrical and battery safety

This project describes wiring mains-powered USB supplies, DC-DC converters,
LED loads, and **lithium-ion battery packs (2S 18650)**. Lithium-ion cells can
overheat, vent, catch fire, or explode if they are damaged, short-circuited,
over-charged, over-discharged, punctured, or charged unattended with
inadequate protection.

If you choose to build a battery-powered variant, you do so entirely at your
own risk. At minimum: use quality cells from a reputable supplier, always use
a proper 2S protection/balance board (BMS), fuse the pack, never leave a
charging pack unattended, never exceed component voltage or current ratings,
and stop immediately if anything becomes hot. If you are not comfortable and
experienced with battery wiring, build the USB-powered variant instead, or do
not build the project at all.
